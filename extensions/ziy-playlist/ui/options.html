What playback can and cannot do here

Playback lives in the panel, and the panel is the player. When you close the panel, playback pauses and your place is kept. The reference keeps playing with its window closed because its player is a browser-side process; this engine gives an extension no such process, so a switch claiming that behaviour would be lying to you.

Streams

An HLS manifest is one queue item, and the panel resolves it with the browser's own media stack. Live manifests are not supported: this engine's streams resolve from the start, and a live one has no start. Such an item opens in a tab instead, where the site's own player can take it.

Quality is a note

Choosing a variant is adaptive bitrate switching, which needs a switcher this engine does not have. Your preference is recorded and the manifest's first variant plays. A selector that promised otherwise would be a control that does nothing.

Playback

Playback pauses when the timer runs out, once. A timer that fired is done; set a new one to sleep again.
