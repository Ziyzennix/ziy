Your own feeds, read in the sidebar and on the new tab page. No catalog, no CDN, and nothing you read leaves this machine.

Why this needs every site

Ziy News fetches the feeds you add, and a feed can live on any host. On this engine an extension page is not allowed to read another site, but its background page is, so all fetching happens there — which is why the extension asks for access to every site. The fetch carries no login and no cookies (credentials: "omit"), so it can only ever read what is public on that page. Nothing is logged, sent anywhere, or shared with anyone: your feeds, your machine.
