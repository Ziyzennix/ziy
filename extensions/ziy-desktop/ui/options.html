What this engine gives Ziy

Probed at startup from the running build, not assumed. A surface the engine lacks is hidden, and the control is replaced by the engine's own path.

    Shortcuts

    These are the commands this extension declares. Gecko binds the ones it is free to bind; the rest are reserved by the engine and are shown with their real state. Rebind them at about:addons → the extension → Manage → Keyboard shortcuts.

    CommandKeyBound

    Keys the engine reserves

    An extension cannot rebind these, and Ziy says so rather than shipping an editor that silently drops the change. This is the honest form of recon S24 on an engine that owns its own key map.

      Your data

      Bookmarks (HTML)
      The Netscape bookmark file format every browser reads, written from the live tree through browser.bookmarks.getTree.
      Bookmarks (JSON)
      Chromium's own bookmarks file, read directly from a Chrome/Edge/Brave profile. No conversion step.
      Settings snapshot (JSON)
      Ziy's own format, versioned. Settings only. No history and no password is ever moved.
